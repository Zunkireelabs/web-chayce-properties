## 2. What the market charges (benchmarks)
Standard removals, 3-bed house, UK, 2026 (industry estimates, not official statistics):

| Source | Figure | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reallymoving (via HOA) | about £792 local (under 25 km); about £1,600 for 100+ miles | Aug 2026 data |
| Sirelo | about £985 local, no packing, inc. VAT | updated Sep 2026 |
| Checkatrade | about £1,300 with packing (about £900 removal + £400 packing) | updated Apr 2026 |
| Compare My Move | about £1,252 for 3-bed | 8,598 moves, 2022 to 2026 |
| MyJobQuote | £800 to £950 local; £870 to £1,000 up to 150 miles | updated Sep 2026 |
| Fantastic Services | £900 to £1,800 | local, up to about 20 to 30 miles |

Labour benchmarks: man and van about £64/hour (one person, range £35 to £93) and about £78/hour (two movers) per Checkatrade; TaskRabbit about £33/hour for moving help.
Decluttering and downsizing help, UK: about £30 to £65/hour; The Clutter Fairy lists £44/hour plus VAT (4 to 5 hours for an averagely cluttered room).

## 4. How Chayce compares
- **Bronze (£995)** sits inside the ordinary removals-with-packing range (about £900 to £1,300 for a 3-bed). It is not a premium price for what is mostly a standard move, so the older-people care elements (DBS-checked team, one coordinator) have to carry the value.
- **Silver, Gold and Platinum (£1,850 to £6,500+)** have no published UK specialist price to compare with. They are above standard removals and rely on the management service. Competitors hide prices, so Chayce's published "from" prices are a trust advantage that families can use to compare.
- **Discovery at £150** sits close to a few hours of hourly decluttering help (about £30 to £65/hour), which is easy for a family to understand.
- Standard firms win on the lowest headline price. Chayce should not compete there.

## 5. Recommendations
1. Keep publishing prices. Specialist competitors do not, and "no hidden costs" is a real selling point for worried families.
2. Show what Bronze adds beyond a standard £1,000 removal, in plain terms on the Bronze page and in the cost blog post.
3. Collect 3 to 5 real quotes from Senior Move Partnership, Britannia and one local removals firm (anonymously, as a family would) and add them to this file. That is the only way to get verified specialist prices.
4. Check the Silver to Platinum price gaps with Chayce's real costs before changing anything.
5. Do not quote any number from this file on the site until verified.
