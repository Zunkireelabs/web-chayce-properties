<h1 class="home-h2">What's the difference between senior relocation and downsizing?</h1>

<p class="body-copy">Senior relocation and downsizing are related but distinct moves. Downsizing typically means moving to a smaller property—often fewer bedrooms or reduced square footage—to simplify life and reduce maintenance. Senior relocation encompasses the broader process of moving to a new home suited to later-life needs, which may or may not involve a smaller space; it focuses on finding accommodation that supports changing circumstances, such as proximity to family, reduced upkeep, or specialist facilities. Chayce Properties supports both moves by handling the challenging parts: deciding what to keep, sorting and packing, coordinating with estate agents and solicitors, and settling you into your new home. Whether you're downsizing or relocating, a dedicated coordinator manages your entire journey, so you're never navigating it alone.</p>
