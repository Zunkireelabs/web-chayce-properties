Why We Exist
12 YrsGuiding relocations

Moving is a big transition — we didn’t think it should be a lonely one

Moving home is one of life’s most significant transitions — and for seniors, it deserves to be handled with the utmost care, dignity and discretion. At Chayce Properties, we built our entire service around that belief.

What makes a move overwhelming usually isn’t one big thing — it’s dozens of small ones happening at once: sorting a lifetime of belongings, coordinating agents and solicitors, packing, cleaning, transferring utilities, unpacking again. Left to add up, it’s exhausting.

We are not simply a removal company. We are a full-service relocation partner, dedicated to guiding our clients through their next chapter with grace, compassion and unparalleled attention to detail.

“From sorting and packing to selling and settling in — we manage it all, so you don’t have to.”
