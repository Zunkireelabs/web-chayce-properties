**Short answer:** Northamptonshire has two councils: West Northamptonshire Council, which covers Northampton, Daventry, Brackley and Towcester, and North Northamptonshire Council, which covers Corby, Kettering, Wellingborough and Rushden. When you move, tell the council for the area you are leaving and the council for the area you are moving to, then update your GP, the electoral register, DVLA and everyone who writes to you.

*General information from the councils, the NHS and GOV.UK. Check each council's website for current services and contact details.*

## Which Council Covers Your Town?

Since 2021 the county has been split into two unitary authorities, each responsible for local services in its own area, including Council Tax and bin collections.

- **West Northamptonshire Council:** [Northampton](/elderly-removals-northampton/), [Daventry](/elderly-removals-daventry/), [Brackley](/elderly-removals-brackley/) and [Towcester](/elderly-removals-towcester/), and large villages such as [Brixworth](/elderly-removals-brixworth/) and Long Buckby.
- **North Northamptonshire Council:** [Corby](/elderly-removals-corby/), [Kettering](/elderly-removals-kettering/), [Wellingborough](/elderly-removals-wellingborough/), [Rushden](/elderly-removals-rushden/), Raunds, [Desborough](/elderly-removals-desborough/), Rothwell, Irthlingborough, Thrapston and Oundle.
- **Olney** is just over the border, in the City of Milton Keynes area, so it is dealt with by Milton Keynes City Council. See our page on [removals in Olney](/elderly-removals-olney/).

## Tell Your Council

- **West Northamptonshire.** You can tell the council that you have moved in, moved out or moved from one address to another within its area, using its [Council Tax change of address page](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/council-tax/change-address).
- **North Northamptonshire.** You can tell the council that you are changing address and moving to, out of or within its area, using its [moving home page](https://www.northnorthants.gov.uk/counciltax/moving-home). You can also tell it if you have bought an empty property.
- **Moving between the two areas.** Tell the council for the area you are leaving and the council for the area you are moving to.

## Bins and Recycling

GOV.UK says your council's website is where to check what household waste it will collect and your bin collection day. See [West Northamptonshire's bins and recycling pages](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/bins-recycling-and-waste) or [North Northamptonshire's bins pages](https://www.northnorthants.gov.uk/bins).

## Register With a GP

The NHS says you can register with a GP surgery or change your surgery for free, and most surgeries accept online registration. Most people register near where they live. See [how to register](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/how-to-register-with-a-gp-surgery/).

## The Rest of the List

- **The electoral register.** [Register to vote](https://www.gov.uk/register-to-vote) at your new address.
- **DVLA.** [Change the address on your driving licence](https://www.gov.uk/change-address-driving-licence).
- **Everyone else.** Banks, insurers, your employer, utilities and broadband. Our [change of address checklist](/blog/change-of-address-checklist-uk/) lists them in order, and our [house moving checklist](/blog/uk-house-moving-checklist/) covers the weeks before.

## If You Are Helping an Older Parent

- Both councils have adult social care teams that can explain support for your parent's area. See [West Northamptonshire](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care-and-wellbeing) and [North Northamptonshire](https://www.northnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care).
- [Age UK Northamptonshire](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/northamptonshire/) is a local charity helping older people in the county.
- Our [moving checklist for older people](/blog/moving-checklist-for-older-people-uk/) covers medication, paperwork and the GP.
