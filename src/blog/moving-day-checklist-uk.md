**Short answer:** on moving day, keep your keys, documents, medication and valuables with you, check every room before the van leaves, take meter readings, hand over keys as agreed and set up a first-night box and the bed first at the new home. Use the checklist below from the night before to the first evening, and print it if that helps.

## The Night Before

- Confirm the arrival time and a contact number with your removals team.
- Check access at both addresses: parking permits, lift booking and narrow streets are the most common causes of a slow day.
- Charge your phone and pack the charger last.
- Defrost and dry the freezer and fridge if they are coming with you.
- Put keys, documents, medication, valuables and a clearly labelled first-night box in one place that will not go on the van.
- Check what is staying. The fixtures and fittings included in the sale are set out in the contract.

## The Morning of the Move

- Eat something and keep drinks to hand. It is a long day.
- Strip the beds last, and keep one set of bedding in the first-night box.
- Meet the team, show them what is going, what is staying and what needs extra care.
- Keep a list of the rooms and the box labels, so you can check them off as they are loaded.

## While the Van Is Loaded

- Stay reachable, but out of the way of doorways and stairs.
- Point out boxes and furniture that must go in last, so they come off first.
- Check the labels: boxes marked by destination room are placed straight away at the other end.

## The Final Walkthrough

- Check every cupboard, loft, shed and outdoor space is genuinely empty.
- Take meter readings for gas, electricity and water, and photograph them.
- Leave the property in the state agreed. GOV.UK says you must leave the property in the state agreed in the contract when you move out, so check the contract if you are unsure.
- Lock up, and hand over the keys as arranged with your estate agent or solicitor.

## When You Arrive

- Let the team in, and show them where each labelled box goes.
- Set up the bed, the kettle and the bathroom first, so the first evening is easy.
- Take meter readings at the new home.
- Check the electricity, water and heating work, and where the stopcock and fuse box are.

## After the Van Has Gone

Tell your council about the move so Council Tax is dealt with (see [Council Tax on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/council-tax)), update your [driving licence](https://www.gov.uk/change-address-driving-licence) and work through our [change of address checklist](/blog/change-of-address-checklist-uk/). Our [first-week checklist](/blog/what-to-do-after-moving-house-uk-first-week-checklist/) covers the days that follow.

## If You Are Moving an Older Person

- Keep medication, important papers and a short list of contacts together in one labelled bag that travels with them.
- Plan rest breaks, and a quiet place to sit away from the noise.
- Ask a family member or friend to spend the day with them, away from the packing.
- Set up their bed and favourite chair first at the new home, with familiar things close by.
