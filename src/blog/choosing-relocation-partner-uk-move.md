## Not Every Removal Company Is a Relocation Partner

There's a real difference between a company that moves boxes from one address to another and one that manages the whole relocation — sorting, coordination, and everything in between. Knowing which one you're hiring changes what the move actually feels like.

## Is Everything Fully Insured?

This is worth confirming in writing before anything is booked, not assumed. Every removal carried out by Chayce Properties is fully insured, with the details set out in the service agreement rather than left to a verbal assurance.

## Will You Have One Point of Contact?

A move with five different people handling five different parts of it is a move where things get missed. Chayce assigns a single dedicated coordinator to manage the entire relocation, from the first consultation through to the final unpack — see [How It Works](/how-it-works/) for what that looks like stage by stage.

## What Happens Beyond Just Packing and Removals?

Ask whether the company can also help with estate agent liaison, legal coordination, storage, and settling-in support — or whether that's left entirely to the family. Chayce's [Our Services](/our-services/) page sets out the full three-stage process: Preparing, Moving, and Settling In.

> The best relocation partner is the one you barely notice working — because everything was already planned, coordinated and agreed on in advance.
