<h2 class="v2-h2">Yes — and they do far more than carry boxes</h2>

<p class="body-copy">If you have searched for help moving an older parent or planning your own later-life move, you have probably wondered whether there is anyone who really understands what is involved. The answer is yes: there are moving companies that specialise in elderly moves, and they work very differently from a standard removal firm.</p>

<p class="body-copy">The difference comes down to what counts as "the move." A conventional remover quotes for a van, some packers and a date. But for most older people, the hard part is rarely the lifting. It is deciding what to keep after decades in the same home, sorting and packing a lifetime of belongings, coordinating with estate agents and solicitors, and then making an unfamiliar new place feel like home. A specialist exists to carry that whole load, not just the furniture.</p>

<h2 class="v2-h2">What to look for in a specialist — and how the options compare</h2>

<p class="body-copy">The elderly moving market in the UK is genuinely varied, so it helps to know what separates a true specialist from a general-purpose service. A national charity such as Age UK is an excellent source of advice and signposting, but it is not itself the company that turns up to sort, pack and move a home. Broad removals marketplaces and large national movers — the sort you reach through a comparison platform or a franchise network — are efficient at transport, yet the person who answers your first call is rarely the person who sees your move through. Dedicated senior-move partnerships do focus on later-life relocation, but offerings and coverage differ widely from one operator to the next.</p>

<p class="body-copy">Against that backdrop, a few practical tests tell you whether a company really specialises in elderly moves:</p>

- **One coordinator, start to finish.** At Chayce the same person handles your move from first enquiry to the day you are settled in — no handoffs between departments. Ask any firm whether you will deal with one named person throughout, and listen carefully to the answer.
- **Insurance as standard, not an upsell.** Every removal Chayce carries out is fully insured as standard, whichever package you choose — not an upgrade you have to request.
- **A plan before you commit.** A Discovery Consultation (from £150, in person or by phone) maps your actual move — timeline, budget and scope — before you choose a package, rather than after you have signed.
- **Genuine national reach from one team.** Chayce manages moves wherever in the UK you are relocating to or from, run by one team rather than a network of local franchises.

<p class="body-copy">Where many competitors are strong on one piece — transport, or advice, or local coverage — the test of a true elderly-move specialist is whether all of those pieces are joined up under a single, accountable person. That is the gap Chayce was built to close, and it is why families who have lived in the same house for decades tend to prefer a managed approach over simply booking a van.
