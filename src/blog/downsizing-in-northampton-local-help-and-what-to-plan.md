**Short answer:** if you are downsizing in or around Northampton, start sorting early, measure the new home before deciding what to keep, check the costs (including any Stamp Duty), and get help with the parts you do not want to do yourself. Local advice is available from Age UK Northamptonshire and the council.

## When to start

Most of the work in downsizing is sorting, not moving. After decades in one house, give yourself months rather than weeks. Our guide on [when to downsize](/blog/when-to-downsize-uk/) covers the signs that a smaller home might suit you, and [Age UK's housing options](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/information-advice/care/housing-options/) explains the choices, from a smaller house or bungalow to retirement housing.

## Sort room by room

- Start with rooms you use least, such as a loft, garage or spare bedroom.
- Use four piles: keep, give to family, sell or donate, and recycle.
- Measure the new home and its rooms, and only keep furniture that fits.
- Leave photographs and letters until last; they take the longest.

Our [downsizing checklist](/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/) and guide to [sentimental belongings](/blog/sentimental-belongings-when-downsizing/) help with the harder decisions.

## Check the costs

Downsizing can release money, but there are costs too: estate agent and solicitor fees, removals, and possibly Stamp Duty on the new home. [GOV.UK explains Stamp Duty](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax), and our guide on [Stamp Duty when downsizing](/blog/stamp-duty-when-downsizing-uk/) works through it. Take financial advice if you are thinking about releasing money from your home; see [downsizing and equity release](/blog/downsizing-and-equity-release-what-to-know/).

## Local help in Northampton

- [Age UK Northamptonshire](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/northamptonshire/) offers advice and support for older people across the county.
- [West Northamptonshire Council's adult social care pages](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care-and-wellbeing) cover support at home and equipment, if health needs are part of the reason for moving.

## Plan the move itself

Book the move early, agree what goes and what stays, and plan the first night in the new home: bed made, kettle, medication and the phone charger. Our [step-by-step guide to downsizing a family home](/blog/how-to-downsize-a-family-home-uk-step-by-step/) has a full timeline.
