## More Than a Van and Two People

A standard removal company gets your belongings from one address to another. A full-service relocation company manages the whole move around you — the planning, the packing, the paperwork, and often the weeks either side of moving day too. The difference isn't really the size of the van; it's how much of the process you have to manage yourself.

## What "Full-Service" Actually Covers

Every full-service provider is a little different, but the core building blocks are usually:

- **Professional packing** — not just boxes supplied, but the actual packing done for you, room by room
- **A fully insured removal** — so belongings, furniture and fragile items are covered in transit, not just "best effort"
- **Coordination beyond the van** — liaising with estate agents, solicitors, or utility providers, so you're not the one chasing every party involved
- **Storage**, when moving dates don't line up neatly between an old home and a new one
- **Settling-in support** — unpacking, setup, and sometimes ongoing help once you've actually moved in, not just up to the front door

## Who Actually Uses a Full-Service Move

It's easy to assume full-service relocation is only for people with complex needs, but the honest answer is: anyone who'd rather pay for their time and headspace back. That includes:

- **Senior clients and families managing a later-life move**, where the physical and emotional load of sorting a lifetime of belongings is the hardest part
- **Busy families** relocating with work, children and school schedules all happening at once
- **Anyone selling and buying at the same time**, juggling two properties and a chain of other people's timelines
- **People moving without local support nearby** — no family close by to help load a van on a Saturday

## A Fully-Managed Move vs. Doing It Yourself

A DIY move is usually cheaper up front, but the hidden cost is everything you have to personally manage: booking a van, sourcing packing materials, coordinating access at both ends, and doing the physical labour yourself, often around a full-time job. A full-service move trades that cost for time, certainty and — for most people — considerably less stress on the day itself.

**Is full-service moving only for large or complicated moves?**
No — even a straightforward move benefits from not managing packing, coordination and logistics yourself; the scope of support can be matched to what's actually needed.

A removal van moves your things. Full-service relocation manages the planning, packing, coordination and often the settling-in around that move too.
