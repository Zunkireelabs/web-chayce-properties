Short answer: decide early, start with family, then choose between selling, donating and collection based on how much time you have. Leaving it to the final week is what makes it stressful.

## Start with family and friends

Ask whether anyone would like particular pieces. Doing this first avoids selling something that would have gone to a relative, and gives family time to arrange collection.

## Selling

- Good-quality or antique pieces may sell through local auction houses or online marketplaces.
- Selling takes time and effort, and not everything sells, so set a deadline.
- Keep photographs and measurements ready if you list items online.

## Donating

- Many charities and charity shops accept furniture in good condition, and some offer collection. Ask what they accept and whether labels and fire-safety standards apply.
- Donation is often quicker than selling.

## Recycling and collection

- Many councils offer bulky waste collection, sometimes for a fee, and some have recycling centres that take furniture. Check your local council's website for current details.
- Book collections early, as they can have waiting times.

## Make the decision once
