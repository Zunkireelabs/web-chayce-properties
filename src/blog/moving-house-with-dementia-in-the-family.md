Short answer: this is general moving guidance, not medical advice. Keep routines familiar, change as little as possible at once, set up a recognisable main room first, and ask the person's GP or care team for advice that fits their needs.

## Plan around their routine

- Schedule packing and the move for the time of day they are usually at their best.
- Keep mealtimes, medication and bedtime as normal as you can.
- Avoid long days of noise and activity around them. If possible, a family member or friend can spend the day with them away from the packing.

## Keep familiar things close

- Pack their most familiar items last and unpack them first: bedding, favourite chair, photographs, clock, radio.
- Set up their bedroom and main living space first, arranged the way it was before.
- Keep important papers, medication and a short list of contacts in one clearly labelled bag.

## Involve them calmly

Where it helps, let them do small, familiar tasks, such as choosing a favourite photograph to hang. Keep explanations short and repeat them gently. Some people prefer less involvement, so follow what suits them.
