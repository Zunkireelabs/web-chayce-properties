Short answer: for a retirement flat, find out the moving-in rules early, measure every room, decide what fits, and plan the removal around the building's access and timings. A checklist spread over six to eight weeks keeps it calm.

## Eight to six weeks before

- Confirm your move-in date in writing and ask the building about moving-in rules: delivery windows, lift booking and parking.
- Get floor plans or measure each room, and decide which large furniture will fit.
- Start sorting into keep, sell, donate and let go. Our [downsizing checklist](/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/) helps.

## Four to two weeks before

- Book your removal and confirm the insurance details in writing.
- Pack non-essentials and label boxes by destination room.
- Notify your bank, GP and other services of your new address, and arrange Royal Mail redirection.
- Tell the building about any large deliveries.

## The final week

- Pack a first-night box with a kettle, medication, chargers, bedding and toiletries.
- Confirm access and timings with the removal team and the building.
- Take meter readings at the old property.

## Moving day and settling in

Do a final walk round the old home, check the new keys, and make the bed first so you can rest.
