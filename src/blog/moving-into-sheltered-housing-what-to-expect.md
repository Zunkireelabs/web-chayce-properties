Short answer: sheltered housing is designed for older people who want to live independently with some support nearby. Features and rules vary by scheme, so ask detailed questions, visit more than once and plan the move around the scheme's timings.

## What it usually involves

Sheltered housing commonly means a self-contained flat or bungalow in a scheme with shared areas, and often a scheme manager or support arrangements. Exact services, charges and rules differ, so check each scheme directly.

## Questions to ask the scheme

- What are the service or management charges, and what do they cover?
- What are the move-in dates, and can I get in early to measure?
- What support is available, and what is it called out for?
- Are pets, guests and overnight visitors allowed?
- What storage and parking is there?
- What can I bring, including furniture and appliances?

## Plan for less space

Most sheltered homes are smaller than a family home. Measure first, decide what comes with you and sort the rest.
