**Short answer:** for an older person, choose a provider that puts insurance in writing, includes packing, gives you one person to deal with and works at an unhurried pace. Price matters, but a cheap quote that leaves out packing and settling in can cost more in stress. Get every answer in writing.

*General information, not legal or financial advice.*

## What matters most

- **Insurance in writing.** Ask what is covered against loss or damage in transit, and up to what value. It should be in the service agreement before anything is booked.
- **Packing included.** Packing a family home is tiring. A team that packs, labels by destination room and keeps essentials accessible removes the heaviest part of the job.
- **One point of contact.** Being passed between a sales team, a crew and an office is confusing. One coordinator who knows the situation makes it much easier.
- **Patience and clarity.** The crew should explain what is happening, check where furniture goes and never rush decisions.
- **A clear written quote.** It should say what is included, what is not and what could change the price.

## Questions to ask before you book

**Price**
- What is included and excluded? Is the quote fixed, or can it change on the day?
- Are packing materials and packing labour included?
- Are there extra charges for stairs, parking or waiting time?

**Insurance and handling**
- What is covered, and is the cover stated in the written agreement?
- How are fragile and valuable items handled?

**The day itself**
- Who will be at the property, and who is my contact if something changes?
- How long will it take, and what happens if there is a delay?
- Will furniture be protected and reassembled at the other end?

**Flexibility**
- How are date changes handled, and with how much notice?

If the company is a member of a trade body, such as the [British Association of Removers](https://www.bar.co.uk/), ask what that membership means for you and check it with the body directly.

## Removals company or relocation partner?

A standard removals firm collects and delivers. A relocation partner also covers sorting, estate agent and solicitor liaison, cleaning and settling in. Neither is better in every case: a simple local move with family on hand may need only good removals, while a move involving a house sale, downsizing or a parent living at a distance often benefits from one coordinated service.
