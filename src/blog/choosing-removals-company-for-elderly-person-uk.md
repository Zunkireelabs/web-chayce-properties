Short answer: for an older person, choose a removals provider that puts insurance in writing, includes packing, gives you one person to deal with, and is willing to work at an unhurried pace. Price matters, but a cheap quote that leaves out packing and settling in often costs more in stress.

## What matters most

- Insurance in writing. Ask exactly what is covered against loss or damage in transit, and get it in the service agreement before anything is booked. Every Chayce removal is fully insured, with the details set out before the move begins.
- Packing included. Packing a family home is tiring. A team that packs, labels by destination room and keeps essentials accessible removes the heaviest part of the job.
- One point of contact. Being passed between a sales team, a crew and an office is confusing. One coordinator who knows your situation makes it much easier.
- Patience and clarity. The crew should explain what is happening, check where furniture goes, and never rush decisions.
- A clear written quote. It should say what is included, what is not, and what could change the price.

## Standard removal or full-service move?

A standard removals firm collects and delivers. A full-service move also covers sorting, estate agent and solicitor liaison, cleaning and settling in.
