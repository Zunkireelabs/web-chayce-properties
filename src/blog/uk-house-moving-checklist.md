## Why a Timeline Beats a To-Do List

Most moving stress comes from things landing on top of each other at the last minute — bookings, paperwork and packing all colliding in the final week. Spreading the same tasks across a timeline, starting well before moving day, makes each individual step manageable.

## 8 Weeks Before: Get the Big Decisions Sorted

Book your removal company or van early — the good ones get booked up, especially around weekends and the end of the month. This is also the point to start sorting through belongings, since knowing roughly what you're moving helps you get an accurate quote rather than a guess.

## 6 Weeks Before: Start the Paperwork

Notify your mortgage provider or landlord of your move date. If you're changing area, start researching schools, GPs and other services you'll need to re-register with. This is also a sensible point to get quotes for removals, packing materials and any storage you might need in between properties.

## 4 Weeks Before: Begin Packing Non-Essentials

Start with things you won't need in daily life between now and moving day — out-of-season clothing, books, decorations, spare kitchenware. Label every box clearly with its destination room, not just its contents; it makes unpacking dramatically faster.

## 3 Weeks Before: Redirect Your Post and Update Your Address

Set up mail redirection with Royal Mail. Update your address with banks, insurers, subscriptions, and your GP. It's easy to forget how many places have your current address until you sit down and list them.

## 2 Weeks Before: Confirm the Details

Confirm your moving date and time with your removal company in writing. Arrange parking permits or access arrangements at both properties if needed — narrow streets and no dedicated parking are the most common cause of a slow moving day.

## 1 Week Before: Finish the Essentials

Pack everything except what you'll need for the final few days. Defrost and clean the freezer if you're taking it with you. Set aside a clearly labelled "first night" box — kettle, chargers, toiletries, a change of clothes — so you're not digging through boxes on your first evening.

## Moving Day: Keep It Simple

Do a final walkthrough of the old property, checking cupboards, lofts and outdoor spaces are genuinely empty. Take meter readings before you leave. Keep important documents, keys and valuables with you rather than on the van.

## What Actually Makes the Biggest Difference

Of everything on this list, starting early and using professional packing support are the two things that most reliably turn a stressful week into a manageable one. Pack a "first night" box separately — kettle and mugs, phone chargers, toiletries, a change of clothes, basic medication, and anything else you'd genuinely want within reach without unpacking everything.
