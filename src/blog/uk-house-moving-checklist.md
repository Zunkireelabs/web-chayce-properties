## Why a Timeline Beats a To-Do List

Most house-moving stress in the UK comes from things landing on top of each other at the last minute — bookings, paperwork and packing all colliding in the final week. Spreading the same tasks across a timeline, starting well before moving day, makes each individual step manageable.

## The Moving House Process in Order

The checklist below covers the practical tasks. Alongside it runs the legal process of selling and buying, which GOV.UK says takes about 5 months on average, and longer if you are part of a chain. In England and Wales the main steps are:

1. **Agree a price and an offer.** When selling, accept or negotiate an offer. When buying, choose a property and make one, after arranging a mortgage if you need one.
2. **Choose a solicitor or conveyancer** to do the legal work of transferring ownership.
3. **Exchange contracts.**
4. **Complete.** Ownership transfers and you move out.

Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own processes. See GOV.UK on [selling a home](https://www.gov.uk/selling-a-home) and [buying a home](https://www.gov.uk/buying-a-home), and our guide to [selling your home to relocate](/blog/selling-your-home-to-relocate-uk-timeline/) for how chains add uncertainty.

## 8 Weeks Before: Get the Big Decisions Sorted

Book your removal company or van early — the good ones get booked up, especially around weekends and the end of the month. This is also the point to start sorting through belongings, since knowing roughly what you're moving helps you get an accurate quote rather than a guess.

## 6 Weeks Before: Start the Paperwork

Notify your mortgage provider or landlord of your move date. If you're changing area, start researching schools, GPs and other services you'll need to re-register with. This is also a sensible point to get quotes for removals, packing materials and any storage you might need in between properties.

## 4 Weeks Before: Begin Packing Non-Essentials

Start with things you won't need in daily life between now and moving day — out-of-season clothing, books, decorations, spare kitchenware. Label every box clearly with its destination room, not just its contents; it makes unpacking dramatically faster.

## 3 Weeks Before: Redirect Your Post and Update Your Address

Set up mail redirection with Royal Mail. Update your address with banks, insurers, subscriptions, and your GP. It's easy to forget how many places have your current address until you sit down and list them.

## 2 Weeks Before: Confirm the Details

Confirm your moving date and time with your removal company in writing. Arrange parking permits or access arrangements at both properties if needed — narrow streets and no dedicated parking are the most common cause of a slow moving day.

## 1 Week Before: Finish the Essentials

Pack everything except what you'll need for the final few days. Defrost and clean the freezer if you're taking it with you. Set aside a clearly labelled "first night" box — kettle, chargers, toiletries, a change of clothes — so you're not digging through boxes on your first evening.

## Moving Day: Keep It Simple

Do a final walkthrough of the old property, checking cupboards, lofts and outdoor spaces are genuinely empty. Take meter readings before you leave. Keep important documents, keys and valuables with you rather than on the van.

## What Do I Need to Change When I Move House?

Most people forget at least one of these, so write the list early:

- **Your council**, so Council Tax and local services are dealt with. See [Council Tax on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/council-tax).
- **DVLA**, to [change the address on your driving licence](https://www.gov.uk/change-address-driving-licence).
- **The electoral register**, to [register to vote](https://www.gov.uk/register-to-vote) at your new address.
- **HMRC**, banks, insurers, your employer, your GP and your utility and broadband providers.
- **Royal Mail**, to redirect post while you update everyone else.

## 2 Weeks Before: Confirm Details and Redirect Your Post

With the big bookings behind you, the fortnight before the move is for locking things down in writing. Confirm the date, address and arrival time with your removal firm so there are no surprises on the day, and set up a redirection for your post so nothing important goes to the old address after you leave. This is also the moment to work through who to tell — your lender or landlord will already know your date, but banks, utilities, the council and your GP still need the update; our separate change of address checklist covers exactly who to contact so none of it lands on top of everything else in the final week.

## Moving Day: Keep Your Essentials With You

However well you've packed, keep an essentials box or bag with you on the day rather than loading it onto the van. Kettle and mugs, chargers, medication, important documents, a change of clothes and anything you'd hate to see go astray are all worth carrying yourself. Because you labelled every box with its destination room, your removal team can place things where they belong as they unload, which makes the first evening in the new home far less chaotic than hunting through a wall of identical boxes.
