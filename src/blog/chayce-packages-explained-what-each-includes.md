Short answer: Chayce has five levels. A £150 Discovery Consultation gives you a plan, Bronze Essentials (£995) covers packing and an insured removal, Silver Comfort (£1,850) adds sorting, staging and unpacking, Gold Prestige (£3,250) adds property search and sale support, and Platinum Bespoke (£6,500) is a fully bespoke concierge move.

## At a glance

| Package | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Consultation | £150 | Getting a plan, timeline and budget first |
| Bronze Essentials | £995 | Packing and a fully insured removal |
| Silver Comfort | £1,850 | Sorting, selling the old home and settling in |
| Gold Prestige | £3,250 | Property search, sale support and storage |
| Platinum Bespoke | £6,500 | A fully bespoke, concierge-style move |

## What each includes

**Discovery Consultation, £150.** A one-hour conversation that produces a plan, timeline and budget for your move.

**Bronze Essentials, £995.** Packing, a fully insured removal to your new home, furniture reassembly, a light clean of the vacated property and a dedicated move coordinator.

**Silver Comfort, £1,850.** Everything in Bronze, plus home sorting and declutter help, property staging for sale, utility transfer management, a deep clean of the vacated home and unpacking and setup at the new home.

**Gold Prestige, £3,250.** Everything in Silver, plus chauffeur-driven viewings, estate agent and solicitor liaison, full home staging with photography, up to 6 weeks of storage and white-glove unpacking and styling.

**Platinum Bespoke, £6,500.** A fully bespoke scope with a personal lifestyle concierge, new area orientation, an interior designer consultation and three months of post-move concierge support.

- Know what you need? Choose the package that matches it.
