## When You're Arranging the Move, Not Making It

Helping a parent move is a different job from moving yourself. You're often coordinating from a distance, balancing your own household and work, and trying to make the right calls without making your parent feel like a decision is being made for them rather than with them.

## What Should Come Before the Logistics?

It's tempting to jump straight to practical planning — dates, removal companies, what to do with the furniture. But the move tends to go better when the conversation starts with what your parent actually wants from their next home, not just what the family has decided makes sense.

## Coordinating From a Distance

If you and any siblings live in different parts of the country, it's worth agreeing early who's doing what, so nothing falls through the gaps between everyone assuming someone else has it covered. A single point of contact — whether that's one family member or [a dedicated move coordinator](/services/) — tends to work better than several people each managing a piece of it.
