<h2>Why the right questions matter for a later-life move</h2>

<p>A later-life or downsizing move is rarely just about a van and some boxes. It involves deciding what to keep after decades in one home, coordinating with estate agents and solicitors, and making a new place feel settled — often while a family is trying to help from a distance. That's why the questions you ask a provider upfront matter so much: they reveal whether you're buying a simple removal or a genuinely managed move. At Chayce Properties, the whole idea is that moving home should feel simpler, and the questions below are a fair test of any provider you're considering. <p>The first question to ask is simple: <em>who is responsible for my move from start to finish?</em> Many removal firms pass you between departments, so by moving day you've repeated your situation to three different people. Chayce Properties takes the opposite approach — every client works with a dedicated relocation coordinator, and that same person stays with you from your first enquiry to the day you're settled in, with no handoffs. The second question is about trust and safety. Then ask about insurance. Don't accept a vague answer: with Chayce, every removal is fully insured as standard, whichever package you choose — not an extra you have to request. Finally, ask about reach. Chayce manages moves right across the UK from one team rather than a loose network of local franchises, which means the quality and the coordinator stay consistent wherever you're moving to or from.</p>
