**Short answer:** in Northampton, start with West Northamptonshire Council's adult social care team for a care assessment, check any home on the Care Quality Commission (CQC) website and visit in person. Once the decision is made with the right professionals, plan the practical move slowly and keep your parent's room familiar.

This guide covers where to find local help and the practical move. It is not care, medical or financial advice.

## 1. Know which council to contact

Northamptonshire has two councils:

- **West Northamptonshire Council** covers Northampton, Daventry, Towcester and Brackley. See its [adult social care and wellbeing pages](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care-and-wellbeing).
- **North Northamptonshire Council** covers Kettering, Corby, Wellingborough and East Northamptonshire. See its [adult social care pages](https://www.northnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care).

The council can carry out a care needs assessment and explain how care is paid for. Our guide on [who pays for a care home](/blog/who-pays-for-a-care-home-uk-where-to-get-advice/) explains where to get advice.

## 2. Check and visit homes

Every registered care home in England is inspected by the CQC. [Search for a home on the CQC website](https://www.cqc.org.uk/care-services) to read its latest report and rating. Then visit, ideally more than once and at different times of day, and ask:

- what the room contains, and what furniture and belongings can come
- whether electrical items need a safety test
- what day and time suit the home for a move-in, and where to park

[Age UK's care home guide](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/information-advice/care/arranging-care/care-homes/) has a fuller list of questions. [Age UK Northamptonshire](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/northamptonshire/) offers local advice for older people and their families.

## 3. Choose what goes with your parent

A care home room is usually much smaller than a house. Measure the room, then choose together: a favourite chair, photographs, a familiar bedspread, a clock, books. Label clothes with your parent's name. Our [care home moving checklist](/blog/moving-an-elderly-person-into-a-care-home-checklist/) lists what to pack.

## 4. Plan the move day gently

- Keep the number of people small and calm.
- Set the room up before your parent arrives if you can, so it feels like theirs straight away.
- Plan the day around their routine, meals and medication times.

## 5. Decide about the house

What happens to the house depends on your parent's circumstances and how their care is paid for. Take advice before you sell, rent or clear it. Our guide to [probate and clearing a home in Northampton](/blog/probate-house-clearance-northampton-where-to-start/) may help if you are dealing with a loved one's home after a death.
