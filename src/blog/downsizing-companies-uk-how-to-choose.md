Short answer: a downsizing company helps with the whole move to a smaller home, not just the van. Choose one by asking exactly what is included, how the removal is insured, who your single point of contact is and whether staff are DBS-checked.

## What downsizing companies usually offer

Services differ, but most fall into these groups:

- **Sorting and decluttering:** support deciding what fits the new home.
- **Packing and removal:** careful packing, labelling by room and the move itself.
- **Selling or clearing:** help with items you will not take.
- **Settling in:** unpacking and setting up the new home.

Our guide to [what a full-service removals company does](/blog/what-does-a-full-service-removals-company-do/) goes through these in more detail.

## Questions to ask before you hire

1. What exactly is included, and what costs extra?
2. Is the removal fully insured, and can I see the cover in writing?
3. Are staff DBS-checked? (See [how we protect clients](/trust-and-safeguarding/).)
4. Will I have one coordinator throughout?
5. How do you handle sentimental or fragile items?
6. Can you work to my timescale if the sale date moves?

[Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/) has general guidance on getting quotes and agreeing services in writing, and the [British Association of Removers](https://bar.co.uk/) lists member removers. Our guide to [comparing removals quotes](/blog/how-to-compare-removals-quotes-uk-what-to-check/) shows what to check line by line.
