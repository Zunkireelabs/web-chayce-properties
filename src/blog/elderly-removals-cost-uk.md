**Short answer:** there is no single price for a senior move in the UK. Cost depends on the size of the home, the distance and how much support is needed, from a straightforward removal to a full relocation with sorting, storage and solicitor coordination. At Chayce Properties, packing and removals start from £995 and a full-service move starts from £1,850. The final price depends on scope.

*General information, not financial advice. Your own quotes are what count.*

## What drives the cost up or down

- **Size of the home and volume of belongings.** More to move means more crew time and vehicle space.
- **Distance** between the two properties.
- **Sorting and decluttering.** Fewer items to move usually means a smaller job, but the sorting itself takes time and support.
- **Packing and unpacking.** Having it done for you adds cost but removes the most tiring part for an older person.
- **Access.** Stairs, lifts, parking restrictions and narrow roads at either end.
- **Extras.** Storage, deep cleaning, furniture reassembly and staging a house for sale.
- **Coordination.** Liaising with estate agents and solicitors, and the possibility that completion dates shift.

## How to compare quotes fairly

A cheap quote and a full-service quote are often different products. Ask every company:

- Are packing materials and packing labour included?
- What insurance covers my belongings in transit, and is it in writing?
- Is the price fixed, and what could change it on the day?
- Is reassembly, cleaning or unpacking included?
- Are there charges for stairs, parking or waiting time?
- Who is my single contact, and what happens if the date moves?

For a longer list, see [questions to ask before choosing a removals company for an older person](/blog/choosing-removals-company-for-elderly-person-uk/).
