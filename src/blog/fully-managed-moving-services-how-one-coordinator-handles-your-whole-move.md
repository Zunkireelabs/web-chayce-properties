<h2>What a fully managed move actually means</h2>

<p>Most people picture a move as a van and a team of movers on the day. In practice, the van is the easy part. The hard work happens in the weeks before and after: deciding what to keep, sorting decades of belongings, packing everything safely, and keeping estate agents and solicitors moving in step with each other. Any one of those tasks can stall a move; together they can feel overwhelming, especially when they land all at once. A fully managed moving service exists to take all of that off your plate.</p>

<p>Chayce Properties is a Northampton-based moving and removals service that arranges moves across the UK. The idea behind it is simple — moving home should feel simpler. Rather than hiring a removal firm, a separate packing service, and then chasing the legal side yourself, a managed move hands the whole thing to one provider. Instead of juggling three or four suppliers who each know only their own part, you deal with a single team that sees the whole picture. That matters most for older people, and for families arranging a move for an elderly parent, but it suits anyone who would rather have the move handled than project-manage it alone. If the concept is new to you, it is worth reading what an elderly move manager does to see how the role differs from a standard removal booking.</p>

<h2>One coordinator, start to finish</h2>

<p>The defining feature of Chayce's model is that a single person looks after your whole move. The same coordinator stays with you from your first enquiry to the day you are settled in — no handoffs between departments, no repeating your situation to a new contact at each stage. That continuity is what keeps a complicated move from falling through the cracks. When one person already knows your home, your timeline and the things that matter to you, nothing has to be explained twice, and nothing gets lost in the gaps between suppliers.</p>

<p>Three principles sit behind every move. First, <strong>one coordinator, start to finish</strong> — the same person handles everything rather than passing you along a chain. Second, <strong>fully insured, no exceptions</strong> — every removal is covered as standard, not an add-on you have to request or pay extra to unlock. Third, <strong>a plan before you commit</strong> — your move is mapped out, with timeline, budget and scope agreed, before you choose a package, so there are no surprises later. And because Chayce works from one team rather than a network of local franchises, that nationwide reach comes without losing the single point of contact.</p>

<h2>The three stages: prepare, move, settle</h2>

<p>A fully managed move with Chayce runs across three stages, each handled by your coordinator. <strong>Prepare</strong> gets the current home ready to sort, sell or move on from — this is where decisions about what to keep get made, which is often the most emotional part of the whole process. Going through decades of belongings is rarely just a practical exercise; it carries memories, and it helps to have someone guiding the pace rather than rushing it. A good framework helps here too, which is why a <a href="https://chayceproperties.com/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/">downsizing checklist of what to keep</a> is such a useful starting point.</p>

<p><strong>Move</strong> manages the practical details of the removal itself — the packing, the logistics, and the coordination with estate agents and solicitors so the dates line up. This is the stage most people imagine when they think of moving, but here it is only one part of a larger plan. <strong>Settle</strong> is the stage many removal firms ignore entirely: helping make the next home feel ready and lived-in rather than a stack of boxes. The full scope is set out across Chayce's <a href="https://chayceproperties.com/packages/">packages</a>, which start from £995, and your coordinator recommends the services that actually fit your move rather than a generic checklist.</p>

<h2>How a managed move differs from standard removals</h2>

<p>It helps to be honest about the alternatives. A conventional removals company such as Britannia or AnyVan is built around transporting belongings efficiently — they are strong at the lifting and the logistics, but the decluttering, the paperwork and the emotional side of a later-life move largely remain yours to manage. Charities like Age UK offer valuable guidance and support, but they are not a single team that physically carries out your move end to end. Specialist senior-move services exist too, yet many operate as regional networks or franchises, which can mean your point of contact changes depending on where you are.</p>

<p>Where Chayce differs is the combination: one named coordinator, full insurance as standard, nationwide coverage from one team, and the genuinely difficult non-removal work built in rather than bolted on. It is not that the alternatives do a poor job at what they do — it is that no single one of them covers the whole move. For a family arranging a move remotely, or an older person who simply wants it handled, that single accountable point of contact is usually the deciding factor.</p>
