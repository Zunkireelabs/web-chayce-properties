Short answer: residential removals are home moves, and what is included varies a lot between firms. Ask for packing, furniture dismantling, insurance and any storage to be listed separately so you can compare like with like.

## What residential removals cover

A residential removal moves a household's belongings from one home to another. A quote might include some or all of:

- Vehicle and crew for the day.
- Packing materials and packing labour.
- Dismantling and reassembling large furniture.
- Insurance for loss or damage in transit.
- Short-term storage if completion dates do not line up (see [how storage works](/blog/storage-when-moving-house-uk-how-it-works/)).

## What can cost extra

Stairs, parking restrictions and long carries can add time, and so can unpacking. Our [UK removals cost guide](/blog/cost-of-moving-house-uk/) and the guide to [comparing quotes](/blog/how-to-compare-removals-quotes-uk-what-to-check/) show what to check.
