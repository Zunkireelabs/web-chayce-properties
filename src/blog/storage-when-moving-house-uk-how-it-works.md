Short answer: storage lets you move out of one home and into the next on different dates, or keep furniture that will not fit a smaller home, without rushing either decision. In the UK it is most often used for a few weeks between a sale and a purchase.

## When storage helps

- **Completion dates differ.** You sell on one day and buy on another.
- **The new home is smaller.** You are not yet sure what will fit. Our [downsizing checklist](/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/) helps you decide what to keep.
- **Work is not finished.** Decorating or repairs at the new home are still going on.
- **You are between homes**, for example while staying with family or renting short term.

## Questions to ask a storage provider

- How long is the minimum term, and what notice do I need to give?
- Is the unit secure, and who can access it?
- What insurance applies to my belongings in storage, and what must I arrange myself?
- Is the unit suitable for furniture, and is it dry and clean?
- Who moves my belongings in and out?

## How to prepare

Pack carefully, label each box with its contents and destination room, and keep a list of what is stored. Our [packing guide](/blog/how-to-pack-for-a-house-move-uk-room-by-room/) explains how to label so unpacking is faster. Keep documents, medication, valuables and a first night box with you.
