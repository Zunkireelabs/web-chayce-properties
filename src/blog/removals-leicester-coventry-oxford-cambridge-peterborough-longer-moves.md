Short answer: a move of 35 to 55 miles is a full day with more to coordinate than a local one. Plan the timing, the access at both ends and the first week in the new home before you pack.

## The cities and the distances

From our Northampton base the distances are roughly: [Coventry](/elderly-removals-coventry/) 35 miles, [Leicester](/elderly-removals-leicester/) 40, [Oxford](/elderly-removals-oxford/) 50, [Peterborough](/elderly-removals-peterborough/) 50 and [Cambridge](/elderly-removals-cambridge/) 55.

## Access at each end

- **Oxford and Cambridge:** central streets can be narrow, with permit parking, restricted access or cycle-priority routes. Plan where the vehicle can stop and when.
- **Leicester:** terraced streets near the centre often have limited parking and narrow frontages.
- **Peterborough and Coventry:** newer estates and suburban homes vary on parking and shared drives, so check before the date is fixed.

## Planning a longer move

1. Allow extra time for sorting, as long-held homes hold decades of belongings. See [how to declutter a parent's house](/blog/how-to-declutter-a-parents-house/).
2. Agree start times so the day is not rushed.
3. Arrange keys, utilities and post for both addresses.
4. Plan the first week: our [first-week checklist](/blog/what-to-do-after-moving-house-uk-first-week-checklist/) helps.

If you are moving to be nearer relatives, [Independent Age](https://www.independentage.org/) and [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) both offer advice for older people. Read our guide to [later-life relocation closer to family](/blog/later-life-relocation-closer-to-family-uk/).
