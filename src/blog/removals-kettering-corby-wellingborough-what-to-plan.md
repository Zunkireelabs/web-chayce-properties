Short answer: Kettering, Corby and Wellingborough are all within about 22 miles of Chayce's Northampton base, and each has its own planning points: access in older streets, decades of belongings to sort, and timing around a sale. Plan for those first.

## Kettering

Kettering is a market town with a long history in boot and shoe making, and its streets mix older terraced homes near the centre with later family houses. Older terraces can mean steep stairs, narrow doorways and limited parking, all of which affect how a removal is planned. Family houses on the outskirts often hold decades of belongings, so build in sorting time. The station has direct trains to London St Pancras, which can help if you are moving closer to family. See our [Kettering removals page](/elderly-removals-kettering/).

## Corby

Corby grew around steelmaking and expanded as a post-war new town, so many homes date from the 1950s onwards. Many are post-war houses and maisonettes, so we check access, parking and stairs before planning a removal. Long-term residents often have decades of belongings to sort. See our [Corby removals page](/elderly-removals-corby/).

## Wellingborough

Wellingborough is a market town on the River Nene with established streets and newer estates. It is close to our Northampton office, so local moves are straightforward to coordinate and visit in person. If your move depends on a house sale, we liaise with your estate agent and solicitor so dates stay in step. See our [Wellingborough removals page](/elderly-removals-wellingborough/).

## Whichever town you are in

- Check stairs, doorways and parking at both homes early.
- Allow three to six months to sort if you can (see the [downsizing checklist](/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/)).
- Compare written quotes using our [quote checklist](/blog/how-to-compare-removals-quotes-uk-what-to-check/).

Your [local council](https://www.gov.uk/find-local-council) covers parking permissions and adult social care, [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) gives independent advice for older people, and the [British Association of Removers](https://bar.co.uk/) lists member removal firms.
