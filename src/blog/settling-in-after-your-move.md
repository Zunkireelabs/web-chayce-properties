## Moving Day Isn't the Finish Line

It's easy to treat moving day as the end of the process, but a home doesn't start to feel like home the moment the last box comes through the door — it takes a little longer, and a little intention, to get there.

## Unpack With a Plan, Not Just a Priority Order

Unpacking room by room, with furniture placed the way it's meant to stay, gets a new home feeling settled far faster than working through boxes in whatever order they happen to be stacked. ## Keep One or Two Familiar Things Visible Early

A few familiar, meaningful objects placed somewhere visible in the first few days do more to make a new space feel like home than a fully unpacked kitchen does. It's worth choosing those pieces before the move, not after.

## Give the New Area Time

Feeling settled isn't only about the house — it's about the surrounding area too. Local routines, a regular coffee spot, a familiar walk: these take a few weeks to build, and that's normal.

> A home starts to feel settled once the routines around it do, not just the furniture inside it.
