Short answer: this UK guide covers the practical side only. Once the decision about a care home has been made with the right professionals, plan the move around ordinary routines, keep the room familiar, and give yourselves more time than you think you need.

## Before the move

- Ask the home what the room contains and what you can bring, including furniture, electrical items and decorations.
- Ask about move-in dates, access times, parking and who will welcome the person.
- Decide who will help on the day, and keep the number of people in the room small and calm.
- Check what needs to happen to the person's current home, such as a sale, a rental or keeping it for a while.

## What to bring

- Clothing in good condition, labelled with the person's name.
- Toiletries and any items the home has asked for.
- Photographs, a favourite blanket, a clock or radio, and a few familiar ornaments.
- A short list of important contacts and papers in one clearly labelled bag.

## Moving day

Set up the room first so it feels familiar: bed made, photographs in place, favourite chair positioned. Keep the day unhurried, and plan time for the person to settle before anyone leaves.
