## There Isn't One Answer, and That's a Good Thing

The cost of a senior move in the UK depends entirely on how much support is actually needed — a straightforward local move looks nothing like a full relocation involving sorting, storage and legal coordination. That's exactly why packages exist instead of a single flat price.

## What Drives the Cost Up or Down

A handful of factors change the price of a move more than anything else: the volume of belongings, whether storage is needed, how much packing and sorting support is required, and whether the move involves any legal or estate agent coordination.

## Comparing Chayce's Package Tiers

Chayce Properties prices its [relocation packages](/packages/) around exactly these factors:

- [Bronze Essentials](/bronze-essentials/) — packing, removals and light cleaning, for straightforward moves.
- [Silver Comfort](/silver-comfort/) — a full-service move with a dedicated coordinator.
- [Gold Prestige](/gold-prestige/) — chauffeur-driven viewings, legal coordination, home staging and storage.
- [Platinum Bespoke](/platinum-bespoke/) — a fully bespoke, unlimited-scope relocation with an ongoing lifestyle concierge.

## Why a Consultation Comes Before a Quote

Rather than quoting from a room count alone, Chayce starts every relocation with a [Discovery Consultation](/discovery/) (from £150) — a proper look at what's actually involved, so the recommended package reflects the real scope of the move, not a guess.
