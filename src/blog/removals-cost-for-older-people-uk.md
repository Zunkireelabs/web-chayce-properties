Short answer: removals for older people cost different amounts depending on the size of the home, the distance and how much support is needed. At Chayce Properties, packing and removals start from £995, and a full-service move with a dedicated coordinator starts from £1,850.

## What drives the cost

- Size of the home and how much needs moving.
- Distance between the two properties.
- Packing and unpacking: doing it for you adds time but removes the hardest work.
- Sorting and decluttering before the move.
- Access: stairs, parking, lifts and narrow roads at either end.
- Extras such as storage, deep cleaning and staging a house for sale.
