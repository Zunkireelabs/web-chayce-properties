## Why a Checklist Helps More Than a Deadline

Downsizing goes wrong most often not because people run out of time, but because they never break the job into stages. A moving date on its own is just pressure — a checklist turns that pressure into a sequence of small, manageable decisions.

## Start Earlier Than Feels Necessary

Begin sorting 6 to 8 weeks before moving day if you can, especially if you're leaving a long-time family home. Set aside time for one room or category at a time, rather than trying to clear the whole house in a single weekend. Rushed decisions are the ones people regret afterwards.

## Sort Into Four Simple Piles

**Keep** — things you use regularly or that will genuinely fit and matter in your next home.

**Sell** — items with real value that aren't sentimental: furniture, electronics, collectables.

**Donate** — anything in good condition that someone else could use, even if you don't need it.

**Let go** — worn out, broken, or duplicated items that aren't serving any purpose.

Working by category (clothing, books, kitchenware, paperwork) rather than room by room often makes the decisions easier, since you can see everything of one type at once instead of judging each room in isolation.

## A Simple Test for the Hard Decisions

If you haven't used or thought about an item in the last 12 months, it's usually a strong candidate for the sell, donate or let-go pile. The exceptions are genuinely seasonal items (decorations, specific hobby equipment) and things with real sentimental weight — photos, letters, inherited pieces. Those deserve their own separate, unhurried pass, not a rushed decision made at 11pm the week before moving day.

## Measure Before You Decide

Before deciding what furniture makes the cut, get the floor plan or room dimensions of your new home. A sofa that looks perfectly reasonable in a large family living room can dominate a smaller space entirely. Deciding based on your new home's actual layout — not just what you're used to — avoids paying to move things that won't work once they arrive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long should I give myself to declutter before a move?**
Ideally 6 to 8 weeks, working through categories a little at a time rather than all at once.

**What should I do with items I can't decide on?**
Set them aside in a separate "decide later" box rather than letting indecision slow down the rest of the sort — you can revisit them once the easier decisions are out of the way.
