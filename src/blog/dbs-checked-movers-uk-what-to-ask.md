Short answer: a DBS check is a criminal record check provided by the Disclosure and Barring Service. When choosing movers, ask whether the staff who will enter your home have been checked, which level of check it was, and whether the company will confirm that in writing.

## What a DBS check is

The [Disclosure and Barring Service](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/disclosure-and-barring-service) helps employers in England and Wales make safer recruitment decisions. A company can say its staff are DBS-checked, but it is reasonable to ask for details.

## Why it matters for a move

Removals teams are inside your home for hours and handle personal belongings, documents and valuables. If you are moving an older relative, the reassurance is more important still. Our guide to [choosing a removals company for an elderly person](/blog/choosing-removals-company-for-elderly-person-uk/) covers the other checks.

## Questions to ask any mover

- Are all staff who will enter my home DBS-checked, including any temporary or subcontracted staff?
- Which level of check was carried out, and when?
- Is the removal fully insured, and is that in writing?
- Will I know in advance who is coming?
- Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

[Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) publishes advice on avoiding rogue traders, and the [British Association of Removers](https://bar.co.uk/) lists member companies if you want a trade body to check.
