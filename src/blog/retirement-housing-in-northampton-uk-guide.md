**Short answer:** to find retirement housing in Northampton, start with West Northamptonshire Council and Age UK Northamptonshire for local advice, search listings such as housingcare.org by location, and compare the types: sheltered housing, assisted living (extra care) and retirement flats or villages. Visit more than once, compare every charge and plan the move around the scheme's rules and your move-in date.

*General information from the sources listed below, not care, legal or financial advice. Chayce does not recommend or sell retirement properties.*

## Who to Ask First in Northampton

- **Your council.** Northampton is in the West Northamptonshire Council area. Its [adult social care and wellbeing](https://www.westnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care-and-wellbeing) pages explain how to get the right care and support and how to keep living independently at home. Age UK says the first step to arranging any kind of care is a care needs assessment from your local council.
- **Age UK Northamptonshire.** A local charity helping older people in the county. See [Age UK Northamptonshire](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/northamptonshire/) for its local services and contact details.
- **Neighbouring areas.** Towns such as Kettering, Corby and Wellingborough are in North Northamptonshire Council's area, which has its own [adult social care](https://www.northnorthants.gov.uk/adult-social-care) team.

## The Types You Will Find

| | Sheltered housing | Assisted living (extra care) | Retirement flat or village |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Housing you can buy or rent with a scheme manager, alarm and communal areas | A self-contained flat with on-site staff, usually available up to 24 hours a day | A flat in a building, or a community of homes, built for older residents |
| Support | Light, if and when you need it | Personal care and support tailored to you | Varies; check the charges and what they cover |
| Inspected and rated? | No | Yes, by the Care Quality Commission | Depends on what is provided; ask |

## Where to Search

[housingcare.org](https://www.housingcare.org/) lets you search for housing and care options. Search by location to see what is listed in and around Northampton. Treat any listing as a starting point: visit, ask questions and check the details yourself.

## What to Check Before You Commit

- **All the charges.** Age UK advises being clear on ongoing charges, such as service charges, as well as upfront costs.
- **The level of support.** Age UK notes that schemes offer different levels of support, so ask what is on offer and what is not.
- **The rules.** Ask about move-in dates, what you may bring and guests, before you pack anything large.
- **The lease or tenancy.** Take legal advice before signing.
- **The area.** Visit at different times of day, and check transport, shops and the GP.
