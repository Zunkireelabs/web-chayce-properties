Short answer: downsizing works best in a fixed order. Decide where you are going, measure it, sort what fits, then pack and move. Starting with the destination makes every keep or let-go decision easier.

## Step 1: choose the destination

Be clear on what you are moving to. Our guides on [downsizing to a bungalow](/blog/downsizing-to-a-bungalow-uk/) and [what to look for in a smaller home](/blog/what-to-look-for-in-a-smaller-home-uk/) can help.

## Step 2: measure and plan

Get the floor plan and room sizes, and plan the furniture before you pack. See [planning furniture for a smaller home](/blog/planning-furniture-and-floor-plan-for-a-smaller-home/).

## Step 3: sort room by room

Start with rooms you rarely use. Use keep, family, donate and dispose piles. Our [downsizing checklist](/blog/downsizing-checklist-what-to-keep-uk/) lists what to keep, and [sentimental belongings](/blog/sentimental-belongings-when-downsizing/) covers the hard choices.

## Step 4: deal with what you cannot keep

Offer items to family, then consider donation or sale. Your [local council](https://www.gov.uk/find-local-council) can advise on bulky waste collection.

## Step 5: sale, purchase and money

Keep dates in step with your estate agent and solicitor, and check [Stamp Duty when downsizing](/blog/stamp-duty-when-downsizing-uk/).

## Step 6: pack and move

Book a packing and removals team once you have sorted. Our [downsizing help](/downsizing-help-for-elderly/) covers sorting, packing and the move, and you can compare [downsizing companies](/blog/downsizing-companies-uk-how-to-choose/).

For independent advice, see [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) and [Independent Age](https://www.independentage.org/).
