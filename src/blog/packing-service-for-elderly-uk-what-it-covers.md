Short answer: a packing service means trained staff pack your belongings for you, so an older person or their family does not have to. Ask what materials are included, how fragile items are handled, and what the insurance covers.

## What to expect

Professional packers bring boxes and wrapping, pack room by room and label what they pack. For an older person this removes the heaviest and most tiring part of moving. Our [packing service page](/packing-service-uk/) explains our approach, and our [room-by-room packing guide](/blog/how-to-pack-for-a-house-move-uk-room-by-room/) shows what is involved.

## Questions to ask

- What materials are included, and what costs extra?
- How are fragile and sentimental items wrapped and labelled?
- Is the packing covered by your insurance?
- Are staff DBS-checked? See [DBS-checked movers](/blog/dbs-checked-movers-uk-what-to-ask/).

## When to book

Sorting comes first. If you are also downsizing, leave time to decide what to keep before the packers arrive. [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) has advice on planning a move, [Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/) explains your consumer rights with a service provider, and the [British Association of Removers](https://bar.co.uk/) lists member firms.
